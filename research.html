Research Sprint

Two weeks of research and planning by the two of us, sold on its own.

What you get

  • A map of the questions buyers ask first
  • An honest audit of what you already run
  • A tracking check before a euro is spent
  • A competitor map of your market
  • Their ads taken apart, one by one
  • The open space nobody is claiming
  • A positioning draft, validated with you
  • A channel plan built for how you sell
  • The one number we'll report every month

How the two weeks run

The sprint is weeks one and two of our usual first month.

  1. Week 1: Research & audit. We study your market, your competitors and whatever you already run, including whether your tracking is telling the truth.
  2. Week 2: Position & plan. You get a concrete plan for the channels that fit your business and the way you sell, not a generic bundle.

What it costs

We give you the number on the call, once we know what you sell.

  • The price is fixed and confirmed in writing before we start.

The research and the plan are yours to keep, whether or not you work with us afterwards. Nothing is held back to make you continue. If we do work together later, read how ownership works.
