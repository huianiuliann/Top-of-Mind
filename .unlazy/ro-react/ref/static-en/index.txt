How you sell
Your customers buy in one of three ways
Before we write a single ad, we work out which one you're in. It changes the research, the offer, the creative and what we agree to call a win.
The quote
Nobody buys until they talk to you. Custom price, weeks of deciding, usually more than one person in the room.
Bottleneck: the gap between interested and in a conversation.
The cart
They buy without ever speaking to you, in seconds, on a phone, off a fifteen-second video.
Bottleneck: how many new creatives reach the market before the current ones burn out.
The calendar
You sell time, and tonight's empty slot is gone for good. Packed weekends, dead Tuesdays, and a platform that takes its cut and keeps the customer.
Bottleneck: demand that doesn't match your calendar, and platforms that own the customer.
Find out which one you are
