How our fee works
Paid advertising
We build Meta and Google campaigns around how your customers actually buy, not around a standard media brief copy-pasted across every account. We start from the research: what stops someone mid-scroll in your category, and what makes them click through instead of past.
Structure built for how you sell. A quote business runs differently from a cart business, so we don't force one playbook onto both.
Creative that matches the platform. What works on Meta rarely works unedited on Google, so we build for where the ad actually lives.
Budget moved toward what's working, weekly. It isn't left on autopilot for a month and explained away in a report.
Websites & SEO
Your site should speak your customer's language and be built to convert, not just look finished in a portfolio. We write for the person deciding whether to trust you, and build the technical SEO underneath so the site can be found.
Copy before design. We write what the page needs to say before we decide how it should look.
Built for the decision, not the scroll. Every page has one job: moving the right visitor to a quote request, a checkout or a booking.
Technical SEO from day one. Structure, speed and indexing are handled before launch, not patched after.
Social media
We make content that shows you know your craft, because people check your feed before they ever call or buy. We build a system around a small number of formats that work for your category, rather than chasing every trend.
A content system, not a content calendar. Repeatable formats that compound, instead of a fresh idea needed every week.
Proof over polish. We show your real work, process and people, because buyers trust what doesn't look staged.
Lead generation
We put your offer in front of the people who are ready to decide, not whoever happens to scroll past. This is where the other disciplines meet: the ad brings them, the page convinces them and the follow-up closes the loop.
An offer shaped by your buying mode. A quote form, a checkout and a booking calendar each need a different next step.
Follow-up that doesn't let leads cool off. The person who was ready to talk shouldn't wait days for a reply.
One number we agree on. Qualified requests, profitable orders or direct bookings, decided before we start.
Tracking you can trust
Tracking sits underneath all four: if a campaign's numbers are right, it's because the analytics were set up to measure them properly in the first place. We fix the measurement before we spend, so every decision after that stands on real data.
Events that match your business. Quote requests, purchases, bookings and calls, each tracked as what it is.
Cleaned up before we spend. Broken pixels and double-counted conversions get fixed in week one.
Numbers you can check yourself. There is no black box: you see what we see.
On your accounts, not ours. Your ad accounts, tracking, domain and website code are yours from day one.
How our fee works
We charge a monthly retainer, and part of our fee is tied to results. What it is tied to depends on how you sell, and we agree it in writing before we spend a euro of your budget.
The quote: per qualified lead
We agree in writing what “qualified” means before any budget moves.
The cart: ad performance
Cost per acquisition, measured against the real margin number you give us.
The calendar: direct bookings
Bookings made on your own site and calendar, counted apart from platform bookings.
Whichever one you are, the same rules apply.
The same number every month. The measure is fixed at the start, so months compare, including the bad ones.
Measured on your accounts. The tracking sits on your accounts, so you can check every figure yourself.
Exact amounts in writing. Your written proposal after the call sets them, once we know which of the three you are.
