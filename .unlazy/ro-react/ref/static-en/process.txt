The same three steps, every time.
When you work with Top of Mind, we research your market, map your competition and build you a sharper position, whatever you sell. It's slower on week one and faster on every week after.
What happens in each step, and what you get
Research the market
We learn your industry from the outside in, before a single ad gets written: what your customers care about, where they spend attention and what makes them trust a brand enough to pay.
What you get
A map of the questions buyers ask first
An honest audit of what you already run
A tracking check before a euro is spent
Map the competition
We find whoever is already winning attention in your space and take their approach apart: what's working, what's copied from somewhere else and what they leave on the table for you. We read their ads, sites, offers and reviews the way your buyer reads them.
What you get
A competitor map of your market
Their ads taken apart, one by one
The open space nobody is claiming
Build a sharper position
We take what's proven, sharpen it and add what's missing across the ads, the site and the content, so yours is the name customers remember when they're ready to decide. Then we launch small, measure the one number we agreed on and move budget toward what works.
What you get
A positioning draft, validated with you
A channel plan built for how you sell
The one number we'll report every month
Your first month
You see the plan on day one and the first real report in week four.
Week 1: Research & audit. We study your market, your competitors and whatever you already run, including whether your tracking is telling the truth.
Week 2: Position & plan. You get a concrete plan for the channels that fit your business and the way you sell, not a generic bundle.
Week 3: First campaigns live. Ads, content or site work goes live, small and controlled, and budget starts following what works.
Week 4: First real report. You get what happened, why and what we change next, written in plain language by the two of us.
