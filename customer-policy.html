Customer policy

Who we are and who this is for

Top of Mind is the brand of TOM BUREAU SRL, a Romanian company based in Cluj-Napoca, Romania, with tax identification number (CUI) 54043345. You can reach us at iulian@topofmind.me or +40 756 883 206.

These terms apply to the businesses that hire us for marketing work.

How we start

Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute call, with no obligation. We ask how your customers buy, where money is leaking today and whether we are the right fit, and we give you an honest answer.

If we both want to go ahead, we send you a written proposal with the scope of the work, the price and the one number we agree to report every month. We start working together once you accept it.

Services

We offer the services described on our services page: paid advertising on Meta and Google, websites and SEO, social media, lead generation and the tracking that measures all of it. What we do for you is what your accepted proposal describes, and any change to that scope is agreed between us.

Price and payment

We charge a monthly retainer, and part of our fee is tied to results. How it is tied depends on how you sell: per qualified lead if you sell by quote, tied to ad performance if you sell by cart and tied to direct bookings if you sell by calendar.

Your proposal sets out the exact amounts, how the results are measured, how and when we invoice, the payment terms and how the advertising budget is paid.

Duration and ending

We don't use long lock-in contracts, so if it isn't working, you're not stuck paying for it. The notice needed to end the work is set out in your proposal or contract.

When our work together ends, we hand over access to your accounts and the deliverables as agreed in your proposal or contract.

What we need from you

To do the work well, we need access to the accounts we work on, such as your ad accounts, website and analytics. We also need timely approvals of plans, creative and pages, and accurate information about your business, offers and prices.

If access, approvals or information arrive late, the deadlines that depend on them move accordingly.

Reporting and communication

Every month you get a report in plain language: what happened, why, and what we change next. It always shows the same number we agreed on at the start, so you can compare months, including the bad ones.

You work directly with the two founders, Iulian Huian and Sebastian Răzeșu. There is no account manager or junior between you and the people doing the work.

Results

We don't guarantee specific results. They also depend on things outside our control, such as your market, your competitors and the ad platforms.

As a guide, paid ads can produce first signals within two to three weeks. SEO and organic social take longer, usually two to three months before the results are meaningful. We tell you what to expect in your case before you commit.

Ownership

Who owns the ad accounts, the data, the website, the content and the other deliverables we create, and when access to them passes to you, is set out in your proposal or contract.

Confidentiality and data protection

We treat what you share with us about your business as confidential and use it only to do your work.

When the work involves personal data of your customers, such as leads, bookings or customer lists for ads, we process it on your behalf and sign a data processing agreement with you, as the GDPR requires. How we handle personal data about you and your team is described in our Privacy Policy.

Complaints and disputes

If something isn't right, tell us at iulian@topofmind.me or on +40 756 883 206. We reply as quickly as we can and work with you to put it right.

These terms and our work together are governed by Romanian law. Any dispute we can't settle between us goes to the competent courts of Romania.
