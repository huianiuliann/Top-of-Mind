First we find out how your customer decides. Then we spend your money.

There's no template industry: there are three ways people buy. Working out whether your customers buy by quote, by cart or by calendar is step one of every account Top of Mind takes on. It changes the research, the offer, the creative and what we agree to call a win.

When a new customer finds you, what happens next?

Pick the closest one. Most businesses are clearly one of the three.

  • They ask me for a price first

    You sell by the quote. Nobody buys until they talk to you, so we work on the gap between interested and in a conversation, and report qualified quote requests.

  • They buy online without talking to me

    You sell by the cart. They decide in seconds, on a phone, so we work on creative volume and a checkout that doesn't leak, and report profitable orders.

  • They book a date, a slot or a stay

    You sell by the calendar. An empty slot is gone for good, so we work on filling dead days and winning bookings back from platforms, and report direct bookings.

The quote: nobody buys until they talk to you.

What it looks like

Every job has a custom price, buyers take weeks to decide, and there's usually more than one person in the room. The buyer is technical, cautious and used to being sold to badly. This is where Top of Mind started: metal fabrication, laser and waterjet cutting, construction and materials supply.

Typical businesses

Manufacturing, custom metal fabrication, construction & industrial suppliers, pergolas & outdoor structures, custom furniture & joinery, automated gates & fences, private clinics and professional services.

Where the money leaks

In the gap between someone showing interest and someone actually getting on a call, not in the ad spend. Most sites in this category make the technical buyer do the work: dig through a PDF catalog, guess at pricing, fill out a generic form that goes nowhere fast. The lead who was ready to talk cools off waiting for a human to reply.

What we build in the first 60 days

  • A site built to start a qualified conversation fast
  • Scope questions instead of a blank contact box
  • Direct scheduling instead of “we'll get back to you”
  • Ads aimed at the triggers that move a technical buyer, not at broad awareness

What we report

Qualified quote requests

Not raw form fills or clicks. Before a single euro moves, we agree on what “qualified” means for your business.

Part of our fee is per qualified lead.

The cart: they buy without ever speaking to you.

What it looks like

People decide in seconds, on a phone, off a fifteen-second video. Nobody here is reading a spec sheet first: they're deciding whether this is the thing that fixes the problem they felt this morning.

Typical businesses

Mattresses & toppers, ergonomic pillows, premium bedding, car seats & strollers, cribs & baby monitors, kids' furniture & textiles, pet products and supplements.

Where the money leaks

Almost never in targeting, because Meta and Google are good enough at finding the right eyes. It leaks in creative fatigue: the same two or three ad angles run until the audience stops responding, and spend keeps flowing to a dead creative because nobody's watching closely enough to catch it in time.

What we build in the first 60 days

  • A steady pipeline of new creative variations
  • Tested fast and killed fast, so winners get the budget
  • A pass on the store and checkout where carts leak
  • Tracking that separates profitable orders from busy ones

What we report

Profitable orders

Cost per acquisition measured against a real margin number you give us, not clicks or reach.

Part of our fee is tied to ad performance.

The calendar: you sell time, and tonight's slot is gone for good.

What it looks like

Nothing here sits in inventory waiting to be sold tomorrow: an empty Tuesday is a Tuesday you never get to sell again. Weekends are packed, weekdays are dead, and a platform in the middle takes its cut.

Typical businesses

Boutique stays, cabins & glamping, aesthetic clinics, private schools, paid courses and studios.

Where the money leaks

In the mismatch between when people want to book and when you actually have room. It also leaks to a booking platform sitting between you and the customer, which takes a cut and keeps their contact details for itself instead of for you.

What we build in the first 60 days

  • Demand aimed at your dead periods, not just “more traffic”
  • A direct-booking path on your own site and calendar
  • Reasons for past guests to book direct next time
  • Tracking that shows direct vs platform bookings

What we report

Direct bookings, dead days filled

The number that shows whether your dependency on the platform is shrinking.

Part of our fee is tied to direct bookings.
